# English Abbreviations Cheat Sheet
## Daily Conversation • Work • Meetings

---

# 1. Daily Conversation（日常交流）

| Abbreviation | Full Form | 中文含义 | Example |
|--------------|-----------|---------|---------|
| BTW | By The Way | 顺便说一下 | BTW, I'll be late today. |
| FYI | For Your Information | 供你参考；告知一下 | FYI, the meeting has been moved to 3 PM. |
| ASAP | As Soon As Possible | 尽快 | Please reply ASAP. |
| TBH | To Be Honest | 老实说 | TBH, I didn't enjoy the movie. |
| IMO | In My Opinion | 我认为 | IMO, this is the best option. |
| IMHO | In My Humble Opinion | 我个人认为 | IMHO, we should wait a little longer. |
| IDK | I Don't Know | 我不知道 | IDK what happened. |
| IDC | I Don't Care | 我不在乎 | IDC which restaurant we choose. |
| IKR | I Know, Right? | 就是啊！ | IKR! That was amazing. |
| BRB | Be Right Back | 马上回来 | BRB, I need some coffee. |
| GTG / G2G | Got To Go | 我得走了 | Sorry, GTG. Talk later! |
| TTYL | Talk To You Later | 回头聊 | TTYL! Have a nice day. |
| NP | No Problem | 没问题 | NP, I can help you. |
| TY | Thank You | 谢谢 | TY for your help! |
| THX | Thanks | 谢谢 | THX! Really appreciate it. |
| YW | You're Welcome | 不客气 | YW! Happy to help. |
| NVM | Never Mind | 没关系；算了 | NVM, I figured it out. |
| AKA | Also Known As | 也叫；又称 | Azure AD, AKA Microsoft Entra ID. |
| DIY | Do It Yourself | 自己动手 | It's a DIY project. |
| ATM | At The Moment | 目前 | I'm busy ATM. |
| OMW | On My Way | 我在路上 | OMW, I'll be there in 10 minutes. |

## Learning Tip

如果你的目标是在英文为主要工作语言的软件开发公司（例如 .NET、Azure、Dynamics 365 或 Microsoft Teams 环境）工作，建议按照以下优先级学习：

1. **Daily Conversation（日常聊天）**
2. **Work & Office（办公沟通）**
3. **Meetings（会议）**
4. **Email（邮件）**
5. **IT & Development（开发）**
6. **Project Management（项目管理）**

掌握以上内容后，你将能够理解绝大多数 Teams 消息、Slack 对话、GitHub 评论、Jira 工单以及英文会议纪要。
