# 11. Meeting Expressions（会议中最常听到）

### Underwriting

The proposal is still under underwriting.

投保申请仍在核保。

---

The underwriter requested additional medical reports.

核保员要求补充医疗报告。

---

### Policy

The policy has already been issued.

保单已经签发。

---

The policy is currently in force.

保单目前有效。

---

The policy lapsed because the premium was not paid.

由于未缴保费，保单已失效。

---

### Claims

The claim is pending assessment.

理赔正在审核。

---

The claim has been approved.

理赔已经批准。

---

The claim was rejected because of insufficient documents.

由于资料不足，理赔被拒绝。

---

### Compliance

KYC verification has been completed.

KYC 已完成。

---

AML screening is still in progress.

AML 正在检查。

---

### IT

The PAS failed to create the policy.

PAS 创建保单失败。

---

The API returned a validation error.

接口返回验证错误。

---

The CRM and PAS are out of sync.

CRM 和 PAS 数据不同步。
